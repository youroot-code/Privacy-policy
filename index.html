Privacy Policy for RootBot

This Privacy Policy outlines how RootBot handles, collects, and protects your information when you use the bot on the Root platform.

1. Data Collection

RootBot collects only the minimum information necessary for the proper functioning of its features (such as ticket support and basic commands):

  • User IDs: To create ticket channels and manage access permissions.
  • Channel IDs and Role IDs: To structure support channels and staff permissions.
  • Message Content: Only messages sent directly as bot commands (e.g., !ticket, !ping) to process user requests.

2. Data Usage

The collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide support ticket creation and management services.
  • Execute commands requested by users within the community.

3. Data Protection and Non-Sale Guarantee

RootBot does NOT sell, rent, trade, or share any personal user data or interaction logs with any third parties. No data is ever monetized or used for advertising purposes.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Ticket channels and their associated permissions are permanently deleted when a ticket is closed using the !ticket close command.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the bot administrator directly on the Root platform.
